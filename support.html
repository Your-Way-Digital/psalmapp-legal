Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my Premium subscription? ▼
On iPhone/iPad: go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Psalm → Cancel Subscription. Your premium access remains active until the end of the billing period.
I purchased Premium but it's not working. What should I do? ▼
Please try restoring your purchases inside the app. If the issue persists, contact us at psalmapp@proton.me with your Apple ID and we will resolve it promptly.
How many psalms can I spin for free? ▼
Free users receive a limited number of spins per day. Upgrade to Premium for unlimited spins, notes, Hebrew text, and more.
How do I delete my account? ▼
You can delete your account directly inside the app via Settings → Account → Delete Account. All your data will be permanently removed. If you need assistance, contact us at psalmapp@proton.me.
Which languages does Psalm support? ▼
Psalm is available in English, Portuguese, and Spanish. Premium users also have access to the Hebrew text of each Psalm.
I forgot my password. How do I recover it? ▼
On the login screen, tap "Forgot password?" and enter your email address. You will receive a reset link shortly. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.
